Emotional capacity

The 4 Gates.
Feel it. Move through it.

A practical journalling exercise for the emotional load you have been carrying underneath the surface.

10 quiet minutes · Pen and paper · Honest answers

Set the ground

Do not push it down. Give it a place to move.

Set aside ten quiet minutes. Take a pen and paper. Work through the four gates in order. This is not about getting the answer right. It is about being honest enough to see the next step.

The practice

Four gates. One honest pass.

01

Isolate.

How do the emotions I have been avoiding truly feel for me, here and now?

Slow down and feel. Really feel. If tears come, let them. Be real about what is going on for you. Write it down. Get it out.

02

Ownership.

My success and happiness in life are my responsibility.

Write the mantra you need most. Read it slowly.

I have all the strength, resources, and ability within me to navigate through this. I am not a victim of anyone or anything. I see the way through. Now is the time.

There is nothing negative here. I have not failed in any way. It is all valuable information to inform my next steps.

03

Exchange.

Build a set of stepping stones to move away from old emotional loops.

When the old feelings come to pull me in, what will I practise instead?

Fear to courage. Self-doubt to self-belief. Frustration to clarity. Procrastination to action.

04

Practice.

Use daily life as the training environment. Refine the process in real time.

Triggered or reactive. Practise Gate 3. Overwhelmed or fatigued. Practise Gate 2 and the mantra. Avoiding or drifting. Practise Gate 1. Stay sharp and focused.

The work

Determination. Persistence.

Practice is the secret sauce. You get out what you put in. Relax. The journey is its own reward. Trust the process.

Refer to and repeat this exercise when emotions overlap too much and you need clarity.
