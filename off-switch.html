The real cost

Never switching off is not free.

We have never lived a more cognitively demanding moment than this one. Staying always on is not neutral. It quietly drives the anxiety, poor sleep, and burnout most people have learned to treat as normal.

Wired

The mind never lands

Even in downtime it keeps running, planning, replaying. Rest stops working because you are never actually off.

Pulled

Attention is not yours

You reach for a screen the moment there is a gap, without deciding to. Intelligent systems are built to make sure of it.

Frayed

Depth gets harder

Holding one thing long enough to go deep slips away. The cost shows up in your work, your mood, and your sleep.
