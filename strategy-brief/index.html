02 / Lead Magnet

First conversion

Make the Capacity Audit the front door.

The audit is the cleanest entry point because it turns Josh’s four-domain method into a personal result. It gives the prospect a gap, language for the gap, and a reason to keep listening.

Everything should point here first: Instagram bio, captions, early emails, and the website CTA while the ladder is being validated.

Recommended

Ten-minute self-assessment. Immediate clarity. Easy email capture. Strong natural bridge into paid offers.

Alternative

Story-Led

03 / Content Strategy

Instagram first

Three formats. One daily rhythm.

01

Outdoor Quote Clips

15-30 second reels with raw terrain footage and one sharp line. Built for saves, shares, and brand atmosphere.

02

B-Roll Voiceovers

45-90 second mission footage with Josh narrating a story, lesson, or principle. Built for depth and credibility.

03

Talking Heads

60-90 second direct teachings. Built for belief shifts, offer explanation, and warm-audience conversion.

07 / Email Sequence

Nurture path

Five emails turn a download into direction.

  1. Here’s your Capacity Audit. Deliver the asset and ask which domain surprised them most.
  2. The thing high performers get wrong. Teach why isolated performance work creates ceilings.
  3. What monastic training taught me. Tell the story that makes the method feel lived, not theoretical.
  4. If you want the next step. Introduce the Field Guide as the first practical block.
  5. The full picture. Introduce the course and mention the 1:1 strategy session.
