Why a constraint, not a score

A system moves at the pace of its weakest domain.

You are one integrated system, not four separate projects. Push your strongest domain harder and the weakest one still sets the ceiling. Find the constraint, move it, and everything above it gets easier.

The trap

You train your strength, not your gap.

The fit get fitter and stay wired. The disciplined get harder and stay unaligned. Effort pools where it is already comfortable.
